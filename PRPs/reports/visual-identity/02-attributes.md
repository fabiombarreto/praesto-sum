## Reading (recorded for steps 2.3–2.7)

- **Brand core in one line:** a precise instrument with a gamer soul — *moderno, ágil, nerd* today; becoming *minimalista, gamer, enérgico*; never *sombrio, sério, corporativo*.
- **Tension 1 — dark but never somber.** The theme is dark-only (plan Q1) while "sombrio" sits in IS-NOT: Praesto's dark is a *lively* dark — near-black base, one saturated accent, warm feedback, light-hearted copy; never gloomy. Consequence for step 2.6: Discord-Onyx-like directions fit naturally; a pure sober dev-tool graphite must be livened; Steam's heavy blue is out.
- **Tension 2 — energy in moments.** "Enérgico" in WANTS while the calm–energetic slider stayed neutral: energy concentrates in *feedback* (complete, capture, celebrate); the interface at rest is quiet. Matches the guidelines' "default to stillness + one signature completion moment".
- **Engineered 78 meets the mark.** The plumb-bob is literally a precision instrument — alignment, exact spacing and geometric type are identity, not just craft. Supports Q10 (keep the concept, evolve the execution).
- **IS-NOT as the cheap filter:** any direction, component or copy that reads somber, stiff/serious or corporate is discarded without further debate (Bloomstein: the IS-NOT column is the veto list).
- **Four neutral axes are freedom, not indifference:** warm/cold, discreet/expressive, timeless/trendy and calm/energetic are left for the three rendered directions (2.6) to explore in different combinations.
