# Life Areas

**Primary responsibility:** the top-level unit of scope — domains of personal life the assistant organizes. The growth axis of the whole product.

**Entities:** Life Area (see `docs/domain/glossary.md`); groups Tasks and Events.

## Business rules (owner-validated 2026-08-03)

- Now: Calendar and Tasks. Leading later candidate: **Notes / personal information** (signaled by the owner's own pain of losing scattered notes). "Never": none declared — the owner considers every Life Area potentially in scope someday.
- Growth is **data, not remodeling**: adding a new Life Area is creating a record — it must never require changing the Task, Event or Reminder entities (domain-model evolution rule).
- Area-specific concepts arriving later (e.g. a health or finance area) attach as NEW entities alongside Task/Event — never by widening the core entities with area-specific attributes.
- A concept that genuinely does not fit this shape triggers a glossary + domain-model change with its own ADR — never a silent workaround.
- New areas enter scope ONLY via the roadmap triage rules (`documentation/50-planning/roadmap.md`) — an idea becomes an FR only when accepted.

## Resolved 2026-08-03

- A Task or Event MAY exist without a Life Area; area-less items surface in an implicit "Unsorted" grouping in the UI (no stored default area) — capture must never force a taxonomy choice (vision principle 5).
- No Life Areas are pre-created at scaffold time; the owner creates areas as needed (vision principle 4).

## Delivered — unit 13 `life-areas`, phase 1: the API (2026-10-11, ADR-0015)

Phase 1 delivered the API only; the *Áreas* screen followed in phase 2 and assigning and showing the area in phase 3 (the next two sections), and the area filter and the close-out in phase 4 (the last section; `PRPs/prds/life-areas.prd.md`). The wire contract is in `docs/api-reference.md`; the pure rules are in `src/shared/life-areas.ts`.

- **An area is a name.** Trimmed, 1-40 characters, unique ignoring case and diacritics (a duplicate is a 409 naming `name`; the route enforces it because the `life_areas_name_unq` index is bytewise and stays only as the backstop), at most 12 areas (a 13th is a 409), listed alphabetically in pt-BR, never bytewise. `description` stays in the schema and the export but the API never writes it; there is no colour, icon, archive flag or sort order, so the unit adds no migration.
- **Rename keeps the id.** `name` is the only editable key; every Task and series keeps its area, and `Casa` -> `casa` (its own name, other case) is accepted.
- **At most one area per Task and per Recurrence Series.** The series template carries it and each occurrence, including a spawned successor, copies it. Re-assigning a series applies from the open occurrence on; `done` and `missed` rows keep the area they had (ADR-0006).
- **Deleting an area is a real delete that loses nothing.** Its Tasks (every status) and its series become area-less in the same `db.batch` as the delete; no Task or series row is deleted and no title, status or date changes.
- **The area-less grouping is a count and a filter value, never a stored area.** `GET /api/life-areas` returns each area with `openTaskCount`, `taskCount` and `seriesCount`, plus `noArea.openTaskCount`; `GET /api/tasks?lifeAreaId=none` lists the area-less Tasks. No default area exists in the data.

## Delivered — unit 13 `life-areas`, phase 2: the *Áreas* screen (2026-10-12)

The owner now has a place to manage his areas. It is client-only: no endpoint, migration or schema change was added, and the screen is built on the phase 1 API and rules. The Task-sheet *Área* control, the area name on the row and capture's pure decision followed in phase 3 (next section); the *Área* filter on *Hoje* followed in phase 4 (the last section).

- **One place to manage areas.** `/settings/life-areas`, titled *Áreas*, reached from a card in *Configurações* (no bottom bar, no header entry on *Hoje*); *Voltar* and `Esc` return to *Configurações*. Creating, renaming and deleting an area happens on this screen and nowhere else (PRD D-G).
- **What it lists.** The owner's areas alphabetically in pt-BR, each with its open-Task count as words, never a ratio (*Nenhuma tarefa aberta*, *1 tarefa aberta*, *N tarefas abertas*), then a read-only *Sem área* row carrying the count of open Tasks that have no area — a plain row with no control, not a stored area (PRD D-O). With no area the empty state *Nenhuma área ainda.* invites the first one, with the add form directly beneath it.
- **Add.** One field, *Nova área*. The screen refuses a blank name, one over 40 characters or a duplicate (case- and diacritic-insensitive, by the shared `lifeAreaNameKey`) before sending anything, with a message beside the field; the server stays authoritative and answers 400 or 409 on the same rules. At 12 areas the form is replaced by a notice that says so. A new area enters the list with zero counts at its alphabetical position.
- **Rename in place.** The row turns into a labelled field with explicit *Salvar* and *Cancelar* — never commit-on-blur — and `Esc` cancels the edit without leaving the screen. A name unchanged after trimming sends nothing; a case-only or accent-only change is sent. A renamed area keeps its counts.
- **Delete asks once.** A single confirmation repeats the verb and says how many Tarefas and séries lose the area (the area's `taskCount`, Tasks of every status, and `seriesCount`), that none of them is deleted and that it cannot be undone. On success its open Tasks are added to the *Sem área* count and a notice names the deleted area.
- **Writes wait for the server.** Nothing is optimistic and nothing is queued while offline (ADR-0003): offline shows the banner, disables every write control and keeps the list readable. A failed write keeps what the owner typed and shows a fixed pt-BR sentence beside the action; the server's own error text is never shown. A 404 or a 409 means another device changed the areas first, so the message says the list was updated and the list is refetched. A failed refresh keeps a list already on screen; a failed first load is an inline persistent error with *Tentar de novo*. If the area a rename or delete was open on disappears underneath it, that mode ends and any failure message it carried moves to the top of the screen.
- **Verification state at the implement-time sync (2026-10-12).** The decidable half of AC-19 is pinned by `test/life-areas-list.test.ts`, `test/life-areas-view.test.ts` and `test/app-route.test.ts`; the rendered screen (48 px targets, the 375 px layout, focus and keys) was checked by reading the code and awaits a live browser pass, and the device items (the back gesture, safe areas, keyboard overlap, overscroll) are owed to the owner's phone. The UI/UX checklist record is `PRPs/reports/life-areas/phase-2/ui-checklist.md`. *(Update, same day: phase 4's pane pass then observed the screen at 375 px — 25 controls, none under 48 x 48, no horizontal overflow, the add form replaced by the notice at 12 areas, and `Esc` in the rename field closing the form without leaving the screen and returning focus to *Renomear*; `PRPs/reports/life-areas/phase-4/live-browser-pass.md`. The device items stay owed.)*

## Delivered — unit 13 `life-areas`, phase 3: assign and show (2026-10-12)

The owner can now give a Task or a recurring series an area and see it on the row. This phase is client-only as well: no endpoint, migration or schema change, built on the phase 1 API and the phase 2 areas. The *Área* filter on *Hoje* followed in phase 4 (the next section), which is what made the observable half of capture inheritance true: a Task captured inside a filtered view lands in that area.

- **Where an area is chosen.** One *Área* single-choice group in the Task sheet, directly after *Prioridade*: *Sem área* first, then the areas alphabetically in pt-BR, laid out on as many rows as needed rather than a sideways-scrolling row. It is the same sheet on *Hoje* and in search; search has no area control of its own (PRD D-P). Nothing is mandatory: a Task saves with no area and *Salvar* is not gated on the group.
- **What a save sends.** The sheet's draft holds no area until the owner chooses one, and the group shows the Task's own meanwhile. Only a choice that differs from the Task's own is sent (set, change, or clear with *Sem área*); an untouched or unchanged area is never sent, because any key on the Task PATCH detaches an occurrence from its series (ADR-0006).
- **On a series occurrence the choice follows *Aplicar a*.** The label now reads *Aplicar título, descrição, prioridade e área a*. *Toda a série* (the default) sends the area to the series template, which reaches the open occurrence without detaching it; *Só esta ocorrência* sends it to the occurrence alone and detaches it. The area counts as changed against the occurrence on screen, so an occurrence already detached with an area of its own never has its series' area rewritten by an edit to something else. Converting a one-off Task into a series carries the area it has, or the one just chosen, so the create-then-delete does not lose it.
- **With no areas, or no answer.** With no areas the group is replaced by one line, *Você ainda não criou nenhuma área.*, and a secondary *Criar áreas* button that opens `/settings/life-areas` from either *Hoje* or search; areas are still created in one place. Leaving for *Áreas* unmounts the sheet, so the Task's unsaved edits are lost (recorded in the checklist). When the areas request fails or has not answered, the group is absent and says nothing, because "you have no areas" would be false.
- **The row.** The area's name ends an OPEN Task's meta line, after the date and priority phrases (`até seg., 18/08 · alta · Casa`; with neither, just `Casa`), as text and never as a colour or a dot, independently of any filter. A Task with no area gets exactly today's line and no "Sem área" marker; `done` and `missed` rows keep today's text and never show the name; an id no loaded area holds (an area deleted on another device) shows nothing. A long name wraps inside the row.
- **The areas are their own atom.** *Hoje* and search read them through one hook, loaded on mount, on return to the foreground and (on *Hoje*) on reconnect. A failure is plain absence by design (AC-22): the Tasks list normally, rows show no name, the sheet offers no group, and no banner or toast appears; a 401 goes to the token gate like every other request.
- **Capture.** `captureAreaFields` decides what a captured Task takes from an area-filter value: no filter adds no `lifeAreaId` key at all, *Sem área* sends an explicit `null`, an area sends its id (PRD D-M). Since phase 4 *Hoje* passes it the active filter's `lifeAreaId` (see the last section); with no area filter the create request is exactly today's.
- **Verification state at the implement-time sync (2026-10-12).** The decidable half of AC-14, AC-15, AC-16, AC-20 and AC-22 is pinned by `test/life-area-assign.test.ts`, `test/task-edit.test.ts`, `test/series-edit.test.ts` and `test/format.test.ts`; the rendered sheet and row were checked by reading the code, the build and the suite and await a live browser pass, and the device items (the 375 px layout, safe areas, keyboard overlap, the back gesture) are owed to the owner's phone. One conscious checklist ✘ is recorded: `Chip` never wraps its label, so a name near the 40-character limit can be wider than the sheet's content at 375 px and make the sheet scroll sideways (an estimate, not a measurement). The UI/UX checklist record is `PRPs/reports/life-areas/phase-3/ui-checklist.md`. *(Update, same day: phase 4's pane pass then measured it and retired the ✘ — the Task sheet's group of 12 chips at 375 px is 343 px against 343, every chip 48 px high, the sheet's region does not overflow, and a 40-character name wraps inside the row; `Chip` stays as is (last section). Recorded in `PRPs/reports/life-areas/phase-4/live-browser-pass.md`; the device items stay owed.)*
- **Copy awaiting the owner.** The new pt-BR strings (*Área*, *Você ainda não criou nenhuma área.*, *Criar áreas* and the *Aplicar a* label) are the plan's proposal and have not been confirmed by the owner; each is a literal in `TaskSheet.tsx`.

## Delivered — unit 13 `life-areas`, phase 4: the filter and the close-out (2026-10-12)

The owner can now narrow *Hoje* to one area of his life, or to what has no area yet, and always sees that it is narrowed. This phase is client-only as well: no endpoint, migration, schema or wire change (`listTasks(filter)` already sent `toQuery(filter)`). The unit stays `in-progress`: the real-snapshot restore drill and the device proof are owed (end of this section).

- **The filter dimension.** `TaskFilter` has a sixth dimension, `lifeAreaId`: `null` is no area filter, `"none"` is the Tasks that have no area, any other string is an area id. `toQuery` emits `lifeAreaId=<value>` last, after every parameter it already emitted, whose order is unchanged; `activeCount` counts it once (the badge range is 0 to 4) and `q` still counts for nothing; `isChipActive` and `toggleChip` carry it through untouched. The value `"none"` is the filter's own and is never mapped to `null` (PRD AC-13, D-O).
- **The filter sheet's *Área* group.** *Sem área* first, then the areas alphabetically in pt-BR, wrapping onto as many rows as needed; one choice at a time, applied immediately, and tapping the pressed chip clears it. The group is absent — not a placeholder — when he has no areas and when the areas request failed, because a filter with nothing to pick, or one that said "you have no areas" about a list that is merely unknown, is worse than silence (PRD AC-21, D-N).
- **The applied chip.** While an area is picked the quick row leads with one selected toggle: the area's name, *Sem área*, or the neutral *Área* when the id is not in the loaded list (an area deleted on another device, an empty list or a failed request). It is never hidden — an applied narrowing filter must stay visible (layout standard §2.3) — and it leads the row rather than trails it, because at 375 px three quick chips already fill the row. Tapping it clears only the area, and focus then moves to the first quick chip so a keyboard user keeps his place in the row. Its visible label is capped at 10 rem with an ellipsis (a name may run to 40 characters, which would push the quick chips past the edge at 375 px); the full name stays as the chip's text and whole in the filter sheet. The decision is the pure `decideAreaFilterChip` (`absent` / `no-area` / `area` with the name / `unknown`).
- **Nothing persists.** The filter is `useState(EMPTY_FILTER)` and is never written to storage: a cold start is unfiltered (layout standard §2.3). *Limpar filtros* in the sheet clears the area together with every other dimension; the row itself holds toggles and nothing else.
- **A deleted area leaves a way out.** An area deleted from another device leaves the filter on an id nothing holds: the server answers an empty list (never an error), the screen reads *Nenhuma tarefa com esse filtro.* with *Limpar filtros*, and the chip reads *Área*. Known edge, recorded and not engineered around: a capture made then is refused by the server under the capture field with the input kept, until he clears the filter; silently dropping the filter would widen the list without telling him.
- **Capture inherits the filter.** `TodayScreen.submitCapture` passes `filter.lifeAreaId` to `captureAreaFields`, so with an area filter the captured Task takes that area, with *Sem área* an explicit `null`, and with no filter the create request carries no `lifeAreaId` key at all (PRD AC-14, D-M). The FR-045 sequencing (clear the field only after the awaited save) is unchanged.
- **Search is untouched.** The search screen has no area control and keeps `{ ...EMPTY_FILTER, q }`, which carries `lifeAreaId: null` (PRD D-P); *Aderência* is untouched.
- **A defect that predates the unit, fixed on the way.** *Hoje*'s foreground refetch is registered once, so it closed over the first render's filter and, on returning to the app, refetched the UNFILTERED list under chips that still said filtered — for every filter since unit 3. It now reads the latest `refresh` through a ref (the idiom `useConnectivity` uses), so the refetch carries the current filter. AC-21's deleted-area case depended on it.
- **The close-out, and what is not claimed.** Chore C6 passed on a SYNTHETIC fixture (rows produced through the real routes on a local worker; `PRPs/reports/life-areas/c6-restore-drill.md`); PRD AC-18 as worded, a production snapshot, was met later, on 2026-10-18 (the Re-run in `c6-restore-drill.md`). The device proof (AC-24) was observed by the owner on his phone against `v0.13.0` on 2026-10-18 (his report: all OK, no further detail recorded; `PRPs/reports/life-areas/device-proof.md`). The overflow of a 40-character name in a `Chip` was measured the same day in the browser pane (`PRPs/reports/life-areas/phase-4/chip-overflow-measurement.md`): the chip is never clipped and nothing scrolls, so the record reads `Decision: keep Chip as is` and `Chip` is unchanged; at exactly 40 characters the pressed chip leaves the sheet's 16 px gutter by 10 to 14 px, a limit case left to the owner to judge, and its rendering on his own device is still owed. That pane pass (`PRPs/reports/life-areas/phase-4/live-browser-pass.md`: Chromium emulating 375 and 1280 px against a local worker with synthetic rows, never his phone) found the two defects the applied chip's paragraph above describes, the lost focus and the quick chips pushed off-screen, and both were fixed at the call site and re-observed in the pane. The invariants inspection and the per-AC status are in `PRPs/reports/life-areas/phase-4/close-out.md`, and the UI/UX checklist in `PRPs/reports/life-areas/phase-4/ui-checklist.md`, with the device items and the few states the pane did not record marked pending or owed.
- **Awaiting the owner, open and not settled.** The pt-BR copy (*Área*, *Você ainda não criou nenhuma área.*, *Criar áreas*, *Aplicar título, descrição, prioridade e área a*, and the neutral *Área* of the applied chip); the glossary and domain-model wording of a Life Area, written in `documentation/` as proposed (PRD Open Question 1) — the "Business rules" bullets above are the owner-validated 2026-08-03 text and stay until he confirms it; whether he wants a *Limpar filtros* chip back in the quick row; the 12-area limit; and whether the 10 to 14 px gutter breach of a 40-character name at 375 px is acceptable or the limit should come down.
