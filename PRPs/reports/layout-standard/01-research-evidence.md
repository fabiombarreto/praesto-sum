# A3 — layout standard: research evidence (2026-08-20)

Three research lenses (navigation models; Today anatomy; state/keyboard/surface placement) plus an adversarial verification pass (32 claims: 22 confirmed, 10 corrected — corrections listed at the end and already applied to the layout standard). Kept verbatim as an evidence record — not maintained.

---


######## nav SUMMARY:
Surveyed nine task apps, Material 3 (including the May-2025 Expressive update), Apple HIG, Android adaptive guidance, NN/g and Hoober touch research, and the CloseWatcher/predictive-back story. Two camps exist among well-regarded task apps: multi-destination bottom bars (Todoist, TickTick, Structured, Any.do's Calendar tab) and single-root/no-bar designs (Things 3, Apple Reminders, Google Tasks since Aug 2024, Microsoft To Do, Tweek). Capture lives at the bottom in every one of them, either as a FAB (Todoist, Things, Google Tasks, Structured, Reminders iOS 26) or as a persistent bottom input field (Any.do Quick Add bar, Microsoft To Do "+ Add a task", Google Keep's former "Take a note" bar); none puts capture at the top. Material 3 is explicit: navigation bars are for 3-5 destinations of equal importance, "don't use a navigation bar for fewer than three destinations", settings are "typically not a top-level navigation destination", and a bottom toolbar and a navigation bar "should not be shown at the same time". Android back for sheets is solved on Chrome 126+ by native dialog/popover close requests and CloseWatcher without history hacks, but NN/g warns against stacked sheets and accidental dismissal. For desktop, list-detail becomes two panes only at 840 dp and above; 600-839 dp is still single-pane (supporting pane 50/50 or a modal side sheet). No primary study directly compares a bottom input bar with a FAB for one-thumb speed; the best indirect evidence is Google Keep's Nov-2024 bar-to-FAB change, which made capture a two-step action and forced a "single tap" setting two months later. Net: the evidence favours option (b), a single Today screen with the capture deck and sheets and no bar, until Praesto has three equally important destinations, at which point the M3 flexible navigation bar (phone) and rail (600 dp and up) should be adopted with the capture deck kept above it.

RECOMMENDATION: Choose (b): a single Today screen with the capture deck as the one elevated bottom plane, detail and secondary flows as native dialog-based sheets or routes, and Search/Settings as top-app-bar icons; no bottom bar for now. Reasons: (1) Praesto has one real destination today (Hoje); Pesquisar does not exist until unit 8 and Android guidance says settings are not a top-level destination, so a 3-slot bar would be padded with unequal items and would violate both M3 ('don't use a navigation bar for fewer than three destinations') and Praesto's own 'bottom bar only for 3-5 equal destinations' rule. (2) A bar would stack roughly 64-80 px of chrome under a 56 px capture field in the thumb zone, competing with the hero; M3 itself refuses to show a bottom toolbar and a navigation bar together, and Apple hides the tab bar whenever the keyboard is up. (3) The most admired single-user task apps (Things, Reminders, Google Tasks, MS To Do, Tweek) run without a bar, and the only field evidence on capture friction (Google Keep) says an extra tap on capture is felt more than the space a bar saves. (4) On desktop (b) maps cleanly onto list-detail: single pane up to 839 dp, side pane for the task at 840 dp and up, without needing a rail until there are three destinations. The trade-off: everything not on Today is one tap further away behind icons (NN/g measured 20%+ discoverability loss and 15% slower mobile tasks for hidden navigation, though on first-time web users, not a daily single-owner app), and the whole navigation model leans on Chrome 126+ close requests (native dialog/CloseWatcher) with a hard no-stacking rule, a visible close control, and full-page routes (real history entries) for deep edits instead of sheet-on-sheet. What would flip it to (a): the moment Praesto has three destinations of genuinely equal daily weight, which the roadmap plausibly produces (e.g. Hoje, Agenda/Calendar from unit 4 if events get their own view, and Áreas from unit 13, or Pesquisar once unit 8 makes search a daily action); then adopt the M3 flexible navigation bar on compact, the rail from 600 dp as already planned, keep the capture deck right-aligned above the bar as M3 requires for FABs, and verify the keyboard behaviour (bar hidden while the capture field is focused). Do not use top tabs as a bridge: M3 tabs are for peer content and Today's groups (hoje/atrasadas/próximas/sem data) are sections of one list, not peers.

######## anatomy SUMMARY:
Screen-anatomy research for Praesto's Today screen (phone + desktop), sourced from vendor help docs (Todoist, TickTick, Things, Google Tasks/Calendar, Microsoft To Do, Apple Reminders, Sunsama, Structured), platform guidance (Material 3, Android adaptive docs, Apple HIG via secondary sources, Microsoft Learn, W3C APG/WCAG) and UX research (NN/g, Irrational Labs meta-analysis). Consensus patterns: overdue as a separate, collapsible top group with a batch "reschedule" action plus inline red date; undated tasks excluded from Today by every major app (Todoist, Things, Reminders, MS To Do, Google Calendar) and discouraged by GTD practice; filters as a chip row with a batch-apply sheet for anything beyond 2-3 dimensions, applied state always visible as selected/removable chips; completion control at the leading edge in every task app, metadata on a second line, swipes present everywhere but always duplicated by a visible control (NN/g + WCAG 2.5.1); read-only calendar events as a distinct stack at the top of the day (Todoist, Things) or lighter/checkbox-less rows (Google Calendar), never intermixed with tasks; desktop = Material list-detail from expanded width (>=840 dp) with a fixed-width list pane, single pane + rail at 600-839 dp, max content width (40-60 chars/line), j/k + arrow keyboard navigation via roving tabindex; header = page title + date, counts only as honest text, no progress bar (progress-before-effort increases drop-off). Recommended anatomy and trade-offs are in `recommendation`; gaps are listed honestly (M3 fixed pane width not confirmed, header layouts of competitor apps observational only, TickTick/Apple HIG pages blocked).

RECOMMENDATION: RECOMMENDED TODAY ANATOMY FOR PRAESTO (phone first, then desktop), with trade-offs named.

PHONE (compact, <600 dp) - top to bottom:
1. Header (flat, not elevated): page title "Hoje" + date line ("qua, 20 de ago") on the left; NO wordmark (wordmark belongs to splash/About/rail top - every competitor uses the page title here). Right side: one icon affordance for the filter sheet with a numeric badge when filters are active, and (later, unit 8) a search icon. No progress bar, no streak, no percentage: the evidence says progress-before-effort increases drop-off and long/empty bars discourage; the honest-mirror equivalent is plain text in the group headers ("Hoje · 3", "Atrasadas · 2") and an earned empty state ("Tudo feito por hoje") once Hoje and Atrasadas are empty. If a count belongs in the header at all, make it the remaining count, not a ratio ("1/4"), and never colour it as failure.
2. Agenda stack (unit 4 hook): today's Google events as ONE group at the top (Todoist/Things pattern), collapsed to a compact stack showing the next event + count ("Agenda · 3"), expanding to rows that have NO checkbox, a leading time column, muted/outline styling and a small calendar glyph; tap opens Google Calendar (read-only, Todoist-style). Never interleave events with tasks and never let them claim the amber accent.
3. Groups, in this order, each with a 1-line header showing name + count: Atrasadas (first; collapsible; header carries the single batch action "Reagendar para hoje" - TickTick Postpone / Todoist Reschedule; rows also show the due date inline in the error colour, Things-style, so overdue is visible even when scrolled away from the header) -> Hoje (primary; NOT collapsible; sorted by time then priority) -> Próximas (collapsed by default, count visible; optional cap to 7 days) -> Sem data (collapsed by default, count visible; Praesto is the only app in the set that puts undated items on Today, so keep it as an intentional 'pick something' drawer, not a default expanded list). Persist collapse state per group; always keep counts visible on collapsed headers (the documented 'my tasks vanished' trap).
4. Filters: ONE horizontally scrolling chip row directly under the header (32 dp chips, 48 dp targets, 8 dp gaps) holding the 3-5 most used quick toggles (e.g. Abertas/Concluídas, Alta prioridade, Com hora) plus a trailing "Filtros" chip that opens a modal bottom sheet (native <dialog>, Android back closes) with the full status/date/priority controls applied in BATCH (NN/g mobile guidance). Applied state lives in the chip row as selected, removable chips labelled with the dimension ("Prioridade: alta") plus a "Limpar" chip; the sheet shows the live result count. Do not use a segmented control for the whole filter (multi-dimensional), though a 3-segment Abertas/Concluídas/Todas inside the sheet is fine. Default state = no chip selected; the view must not remember a narrowing filter across cold starts without showing it.
5. Rows: 64 px (project constant; note it sits between M3 56/72 - if the metadata line is always present, 72 dp is the Material-correct height; if metadata is optional, 64 is a defensible compromise - flag for the design pass). Leading 48x48 completion circle (every task app puts it leading; M3 allows it), title single-line truncated, second line metadata in this order: due time · area colour dot/name · priority glyph; overdue date in error colour. Trailing slot reserved for ONE thing only (priority flag or reminder bell), never a chevron. Swipe right = concluir, swipe left = agendar (Things/Todoist defaults), with: visible alternatives for both (checkbox; tap row -> detail sheet with date controls; long-press -> context menu), a first-run hint, confirmation-free but undoable completion via toast (NN/g), and no swipe for delete.
6. Capture deck: bottom-anchored, the one elevated plane, 56 px field (given). The filter sheet and detail sheet are the only overlays; both close on Android back.
Navigation: given 'bottom bar only for 3-5 equal destinations', option (b) single screen + sheets fits units 3-8 better than a bar with Hoje · Pesquisar · Configurações (search and settings are not peers of Hoje). Revisit at unit 13 (life areas) when real peer destinations appear. Trade-off: (b) hides search behind an icon; mitigate with a pull-down-to-search gesture later (Things) only if a visible icon also exists.

DESKTOP / WINDOWS PWA:
- 600-839 dp (medium): single pane + left navigation rail (M3 medium single-pane rule), list column capped at ~600-720 px (40-60 chars per line) and centred; detail opens as a side sheet from the right; capture deck stays bottom-anchored inside the column. Nothing stretches (Android 'set a max width' rule).
- >=840 dp (expanded; a Windows snap-half at 1920 px = 960 px lands here, and most laptop windows are Large >=1008 epx): Material list-detail. Left: rail with wordmark at top. Middle: fixed-width list pane (recommend 380-420 px; M3 publishes no number - see gaps) containing the same header, agenda stack, groups and chip row. Right: flexible detail pane that is FLAT (no elevation) so the capture deck stays the single elevated plane; empty state placeholder when nothing is selected; Esc clears selection; narrowing below 840 keeps the detail visible and hides the list (Android rule), back returns to the list.
- Capture on desktop: keep it bottom-anchored in the list pane for model consistency with the phone, but give it a global shortcut (Q or N, Todoist-style) so keyboard users never reach for it. Trade-off: bottom placement is slightly unusual on desktop; a top placement would be more conventional but breaks the 'capture is the hero in the thumb zone' identity. Decide in sketches; the shortcut makes either choice workable.
- Keyboard: rows form one tab stop with roving tabindex; Up/Down and J/K move focus, Home/End jump, Enter opens detail, Space or E toggles complete, T opens schedule, 1-4 set priority, Esc closes, / focuses search, G then H (Hoje) for navigation. Use the APG grid semantics when a row has more than one focusable control, otherwise listbox.
- Windows conventions: ship with the default title bar first; Window Controls Overlay is optional polish (adds env(titlebar-area-*), app-region: drag, geometrychange debouncing and a draggable header) - defer until the layout is stable. Keep all sizes in multiples of 4 px; treat <640 px windows as compact (phone layout, not a squeezed desktop).

OVERALL TRADE-OFFS: four groups + agenda + chips on one phone screen strains 'few elements per screen' - the default-collapsed Próximas/Sem data and the collapsed agenda stack are what keep Hoje and the capture deck as the only expanded elements; collapsible groups need persisted state and always-visible counts or they create 'missing task' confusion; swipes must be duplicated by visible controls (cost: more UI) or dropped (cost: speed); list-detail adds a second surface that must stay visually flat to honour the single-elevated-plane rule; 64 px rows are a project constant, not a Material value.

RECOMMENDATION: KEYBOARD DECISION — adopt `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, viewport-fit=cover, interactive-widget=resizes-content">` as the mechanism, not VisualViewport JS and not the VirtualKeyboard API. Rationale: it is the web equivalent of Android's adjustResize (the layout viewport shrinks, so the 56 px capture field and anything anchored to it ride above the keyboard with zero script), it works in Chrome 108+/Firefox 132+ (the only Android engines Praesto targets), the startup clientHeight bug is fixed in Chrome 139, desktop has no OSK so nothing changes there, and the VirtualKeyboard API is Chromium-only and documented as buggy in March 2026. Required hygiene: build the shell as a `100dvh` grid (header / scrolling list / capture deck) with `overflow: clip` on the shell, never use height-based media queries (width-based ≥600 dp rail breakpoint is safe), give the list its own scroll container so the focused field never triggers a document scroll, and drop `safe-area-inset-bottom` from the deck while a field is focused (`:root:has(input:focus-visible, textarea:focus-visible)`) to avoid the gap above the keyboard.
