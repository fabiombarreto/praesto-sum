## Candidate Decisions (for operator review)

The following were observed in the diff but are implementation latitude the PRD and plan left open, or are inferred, so they were NOT written to `docs/decisions.md` or `docs/anti-patterns.md` per the PRESERVE-ENTIRELY rule:

- **The delete confirmation counts all of an area's Tasks, while its row counts only the open ones.** The row says `formatOpenTaskCount(openTaskCount)` (*2 tarefas abertas*); the confirmation says how many Tarefas and séries lose the area from `taskCount` (Tasks of every status) and `seriesCount` (*5 tarefas e 1 série ficam sem área*). PRD AC-19 and D-J ask for "how many Tarefas and séries lose the area", so this is the specified behaviour, but the two numbers on one screen can differ and the owner may read it as a mismatch. If the owner wants the confirmation to say "abertas" or to show both, that is a copy decision for him, not one to infer.
- **A screen keeps one `primary` action: *Adicionar* is rendered `secondary` while a rename form (with its own primary *Salvar*) is open.** The plan disclosed this addition; it applies guidelines §3.1 across two forms on one screen rather than adding a rule. If the owner wants the two-forms case written down, `documentation/40-engineering/ui-ux-guidelines.md` is where it belongs; it is not a project decision as it stands.
- **Nested `Esc`: a field that handles `Esc` calls `preventDefault()` and the screen's `window` listener returns early on `defaultPrevented`, so one key press cancels the edit and does not also leave the screen.** The layout standard's keyboard bullet lists only `Esc`, `/` and `Enter` on an inline title; this is a reusable pattern the owner may or may not want recorded there. Not promoted.
- **Possible anti-pattern: "a per-row mode (rename, delete confirmation) keyed by row id that is not reset when its row leaves the list".** Code-review attempt 1 found it on this screen (the demoted primary was left with no *Salvar* on screen and the failure message died with the row) and the fix is a render-phase reset that moves a carried message to the top. It is a defect found and fixed in one component, not a stated project-wide prohibition, so it is a candidate only.
