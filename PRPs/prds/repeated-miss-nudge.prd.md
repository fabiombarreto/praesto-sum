## Problem Statement

The owner keeps failing the same recurring Task — the gym every week, the daily reading — and the only surfaces that
say so are passive: a line on *Hoje* and the *Aderência* route, both of which need him to go and look. ADR-0006
records his own words ("if I keep failing the same tasks … I must be notified"), and FR-012 promised exactly that
notification, with "threshold and cadence defined at design time". Today nothing tells him at the moment a pattern
is confirmed, so a series can sit at six misses in a row unnoticed; and a naive fix (a push for every miss) would
be the opposite failure — noise he mutes at the operating-system level, taking his real Reminders with it.

## Evidence

- `documentation/20-requirements/functional-requirements.md:45` — FR-012 (Should, accepted): "When the same
  recurring Task is missed repeatedly, the assistant proactively notifies the owner (threshold and cadence
  defined at design time)." Traceability (`:118`) assigns it to this unit.
- `documentation/60-decisions/ADR-0006-recurrence-model.md:21` — the owner (2026-08-03): "if I keep failing the
  same tasks while using the app correctly, I must be notified and constantly see my misses; then it is on me to
  replan or change my behavior."
- `documentation/50-planning/roadmap.md:65` — unit 12's own exit signal: after N misses the owner got exactly ONE
  notification about it that week, and silenced that series without losing the other reminders.
- The aggregation the nudge needs now exists: unit 11 shipped the per-series record (`src/shared/adherence.ts`,
  `loadSeriesAdherence` in `src/worker/routes/series.ts:266-302`). It has **no** "consecutive misses" count —
  `currentStreak` counts trailing `done`, `recentMisses` is a 30-day window, `missed_count` is a lifetime total
  (`src/worker/db/schema.ts:88`).
- The send path is proven: the phone rang with the app closed (unit 6, 2026-09-09) and a deep link to a Task
  opened the right sheet (unit 7, 2026-09-15). The claim-then-send sweep (`src/worker/cron.ts:196-256`) is the
  template for "exactly once".
- Market scan (weak evidence, see Research Summary): no surveyed habit app escalates after N misses; the
  patterns found are to log a miss once rather than re-ping, to mute per item, and to use a notification `tag`
  so a repeat replaces rather than stacks.

## Proposed Solution

When a recurring Task series reaches **three consecutive `missed` occurrences**, the cron sends the owner **one**
push notification that opens that series' open occurrence directly — where he can already *Encerrar repetição*,
edit the rule or replan — and then stays silent about that run of misses, however long it continues. The
notification re-arms only after he completes an occurrence. A per-series switch, in that same sheet, silences
the nudge for one series without touching any Reminder. The threshold is a constant in code, not a setting: one
owner, one decision, no configuration surface to maintain. This reuses the shipped send path and the
claim-before-send pattern rather than inventing a queue.

## Key Hypothesis

We believe **one** push the moment a series reaches three consecutive non-completions, linked straight to that
series, will make the owner replan or end a commitment he keeps failing — more than the passive screen does —
without becoming noise he mutes. We'll know we're right when, after three consecutive misses of a real series, the
owner receives exactly one notification, opens it onto the series, and either replans, ends it or silences it —
and no second notification arrives for that run.

## What We're NOT Building

- **A configurable threshold or cadence** — one owner; N = 3 is a constant. A setting is a surface to maintain
  for a decision he makes once.
- **Escalation, repetition or a daily/weekly digest** — "not daily, not forever" is the requirement; the market
  scan found no pattern that repeats after N without fatigue.
- **Snooze** — silencing is per series and persistent until he turns it back on; a timed snooze is a second state
  machine for no stated need.
- **Silencing from the notification itself** — a notification action would have the service worker call the API
  with the bearer token; the sheet it opens already hosts the switch.
- **Nudging about a one-off Task, an ended series or an Event** — only an active recurring *Task* series.
- **Any change to Reminders** — a muted series keeps every Reminder of its Tasks; muting is about this nudge only.
- **A new route for "the series"** — the notification opens the open occurrence's existing Task route.
- **A retroactive nudge when a series is unmuted** — see D-I; unmuting never fires a notification by itself.

## Acceptance Criteria (test scenarios)

- **AC-1 Consecutive count:** Given a series' closed occurrences ordered by `occurrence_date` ending
  `done, missed, missed, missed`, when the trailing consecutive-miss count is computed, then it is 3; given
  `missed, missed, done, missed`, it is 1; given only open occurrences or none, it is 0.
- **AC-2 A completion resets it:** Given `missed, missed, missed, done`, when counted, then 0; and given the series
  then misses twice more, it is 2.
- **AC-3 Skipped cycles leave no row and do not interfere:** Given `missed, missed` with a deleted (skipped) open
  occurrence between them and a third `missed` after, when counted, then 3 — the same reading ADR-0006 and the
  adherence streak apply (AC-5 of `adherence-mirror`).
- **AC-4 Due decision:** Given an `active` task series, not muted, `nudged_at` null, with a trailing count ≥ 3,
  when the nudge decision runs at a local time ≥ 09:00 in the series' time zone, then it is due; given any one of
  — `ended`, muted, `nudged_at` set, count 2 — then it is not.
- **AC-5 Time-of-day gate, per series zone:** Given the sweep marked the third miss at 00:05 local, when the cron
  ticks at 00:10, 03:00 and 08:55, then nothing is sent; when it ticks at 09:00 or later the same day, then
  exactly one is sent. For a series in `UTC` and another in `America/Sao_Paulo` at the same instant, each is
  judged on its own local hour.
- **AC-6 Exactly once under overlap:** Given a due nudge and two cron invocations racing, when both run, then
  exactly one claims it (conditional update on `nudged_at IS NULL`) and exactly one push is dispatched.
- **AC-7 No repetition while the run continues:** Given a series nudged at three misses, when a 4th and 5th
  consecutive miss are recorded and the cron runs, then no further notification is sent.
- **AC-8 Re-arm on completion:** Given a nudged series, when an occurrence is completed (`done`), then
  `nudged_at` is cleared; and when three further consecutive misses accrue, then exactly one new notification is
  sent.
- **AC-9 Payload and deep link:** Given a due nudge, when the payload is built, then the title is ≤ 30
  characters, the `tag` is per series (a repeat for the same series would replace, not stack), `data.route` is
  the path of the series' **open occurrence** (`/tasks/<id>`), and the copy is pt-BR naming the series and the
  count and never containing "falha", "falhando" or "pulada". A series with no open occurrence is not nudged.
- **AC-10 Send outcomes:** Given a due nudge, when a subscription answers 404/410, then that subscription is
  pruned; when the outcome is retryable (429/5xx/no status), then the claim is released so the next cron tick
  retries; when no subscription exists, then the series is **not** marked nudged (the owner can still be told
  after subscribing).
- **AC-11 Mute is per series and leaves Reminders alone:** Given two series each with a Reminder, when one is
  muted via `PATCH /api/series/:id` `{ nudgeMuted: true }`, then no nudge is sent for it, the other series is
  still nudged, and both series' Reminders still fire. A non-boolean `nudgeMuted` is a 400 and writes nothing;
  an unknown extra key keeps the route's existing rejection.
- **AC-12 A muted run is consumed, not queued (D-I):** Given a muted series that reaches three consecutive
  misses, when the cron runs, then `nudged_at` is set **without** sending; when the owner later unmutes it, then
  no notification is sent for that run, and a *new* run of three after a completion nudges normally.
- **AC-13 A nudge failure never blocks Reminders:** Given the nudge step throws, when `runScheduledJob` runs,
  then the due-Reminder sweep still executes and the cron heartbeat records the failure in `cron_runs`, the same
  deferred-error behaviour the missed sweep already has.
- **AC-14 Migration and export:** Given the new migration is applied to a database with existing series, then
  every existing row keeps its data, `nudge_muted` defaults to false and `nudged_at` to null; and the FR-042
  export and its restore drill carry both columns, so the owner's mute survives a restore.
- **AC-15 The control in the sheet:** Given the sheet of an occurrence of an active series, when it opens, then a
  switch for the nudge shows its state in pt-BR and toggling it saves through the series route; an occurrence of
  an ended series or a one-off Task shows no such control; and the *Aderência* route marks a silenced series
  with *Avisos silenciados* in its row.
- **AC-16 UI/UX checklist recorded:** Given the change touches `src/app/`, when the Implementer finishes, then the
  review checklist of `documentation/40-engineering/ui-ux-guidelines.md` has a recorded ✔/✘ result before the
  merge, and no visible string leaves pt-BR.
- **AC-17 Device proof (manual, owner-observed):** Given production with this unit deployed and a real daily or
  weekly series the owner lets lapse for three consecutive occurrences, when the third is recorded and the
  09:00 window arrives, then his phone shows exactly one notification; tapping it opens the series' open
  occurrence; silencing that series prevents any further one; and his Reminders are unaffected. Recorded in
  Delivery history by him.

## Open Questions

- [ ] **Unmute behaviour (D-I)** — the default chosen here is that a muted run is *consumed* and unmuting never
  fires a retroactive nudge. Confirm, or choose "unmuting a series still at ≥ 3 nudges at the next window".
- [ ] **Upper bound of the time window** — the owner agreed "from 09:00" with no upper bound; a miss recorded at
  23:50 local could therefore notify at 23:55. Accept, or add an end (e.g. 21:00 → next morning).
- [ ] **First-day burst** — every series already at ≥ 3 consecutive misses when this ships is nudged once, one
  notification each (distinct tags). Accept for a handful of series, or backfill `nudged_at` so only *new* runs
  nudge.
- [ ] **iOS/PWA delivery of a `tag`-replaced notification** — not confirmed by the market scan; verify on the
  owner's installed device during AC-17.

## Users & Context

**Non-Users**
Anyone else (single-user system, ADR-0003); and the owner himself in the case of one-off Tasks, which never
recur and are not "missed repeatedly".

### MVP Scope

Phases 1–4 below: the pure count and decision, the two columns and the PATCH, the cron step, and the sheet
switch with its device proof. Nothing else.

### User Flow

The owner lets a daily series lapse → the sweep records the third consecutive `missed` → at the first cron tick
from 09:00 local one push arrives: "*<série>*: 3 não concluídas seguidas" → he taps it → the series' open
occurrence opens, with the repetition section → he edits the rule, ends the series, or flips the nudge switch off.

## Technical Approach

**Feasibility:** HIGH — every moving part exists (cron, send path, claim pattern, deep link, series PATCH, sheet
series section); the new surface is two columns, one pure function and one cron step.

### TDD routing

Current value of `tdd` in `docs/context/methodology.md`: **true**. Test-first ordering — the test pair
(test-writer/test-reviewer) produces the initial test suite from the Acceptance Criteria above, before the
Implementer runs. AC-15..AC-17 are the manual/UI half and produce no test file, per the methodology's "UI stays
manually verified" line.

### Architecture Notes

- **Pure core in `src/shared/nudge.ts`:** `trailingMissStreak(rows)` and `decideNudge(series, rows, now)`; no
  clock reads, no DB, no imports from the Worker. `src/shared/recurrence.ts` and `src/shared/adherence.ts` stay
  unchanged. It reads the same closed rows `loadSeriesAdherence` does, grouped the same way.
- **Two columns on `recurrence_series`:** `nudged_at integer null` (timestamp, system state) and `nudge_muted
  integer boolean not null default false` (owner preference). A migration generated by drizzle-kit (`0006`),
  applied by wrangler; both classified in `src/worker/db/export-manifest.ts` so the export and the restore drill
  carry them (AC-14).
- **Claim before send:** the cron step selects due series, then sets `nudged_at` with a conditional `UPDATE ...
  WHERE id = ? AND nudged_at IS NULL` and dispatches only if the row came back — the exact shape of the
  due-Reminder sweep (`src/worker/cron.ts:196-256`); a retryable outcome clears the claim.
- **Where it runs:** immediately after `sweepMissedOccurrences` and before/alongside the Reminder sweep inside
  `runScheduledJob`; an error is deferred and rethrown after Reminders go out, as the missed sweep already does,
  so `runCronHeartbeat` records it (AC-13).
- **Per-series local time:** the 09:00 gate reads the series' own time zone with the same fallback to
  `America/Sao_Paulo` that `todayForSeries` uses for an unresolvable zone.
- **Completion clears the marker:** wherever an occurrence of a series is closed `done` (the successor-spawn path
  in `src/worker/successor.ts`), `nudged_at` is cleared in the same batch.
- **Payload:** `buildNotificationPayload({ title, body, route, tag })` with `route` = `pathOf(taskRouteOf(openId))`
  and `tag` = `nudge-<seriesId>`; no new `AppRoute` member.
- **Wire contract:** `SeriesDto` gains `nudgeMuted: boolean`; `PATCH /api/series/:id` accepts `nudgeMuted` through
  its closed `EDITABLE_SERIES_FIELDS` allowlist, validated as a boolean. `nudged_at` is not exposed.

### Technical Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|------------|------------|
| Duplicate nudge from two overlapping cron runs | M | Conditional-update claim; AC-6 simulates the race |
| A nudge is lost because the claim was taken and the send hard-crashed | L | Same residual as Reminders (ADR 2026-09-10); `cron_runs` makes the crash visible; the next run of misses re-arms |
| Time-zone or DST error in the 09:00 gate | M | Pure function takes `now` and the zone; tests at both sides of the boundary and for two zones (AC-5) |
| First-day burst of notifications for old runs | M | Accepted by default (open question); silencing is one tap per series |
| Unmuting surprises with a retroactive notification | L | D-I consumes a muted run silently (AC-12) |
| iOS/PWA does not replace a same-`tag` notification | L | Not required for correctness (the claim guarantees one send); verified on the device in AC-17 |

## Research Summary

**Market Context** (web research; weak evidence — see gaps)
- Constanza logs an unanswered slot as "Missed" once at midnight and feeds it into streaks rather than re-pinging;
  an optional nightly review lists what is still open — https://f-droid.org/packages/com.jjrapps.constanza/
- Loop Habit Tracker gives each habit its own reminder and snooze, and decays a score instead of a hard reset —
  https://f-droid.org/en/packages/org.isoron.uhabits
- Habitify's follow-up streak notification fires ~30 minutes after the reminder and has no independent mute; the
  workaround is turning that habit's reminder off — https://intercom.help/habitify-app/en/articles/13459957-streak-update-notification
- `showNotification`'s `tag` replaces a prior notification and `renotify` controls re-alerting (default false) —
  https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/ServiceWorkerRegistration/showNotification
- iOS web push needs the app on the Home Screen, requests permission only on a user gesture, and is controlled
  per app and by Focus — https://webkit.org/blog/13878/web-push-for-web-apps-on-ios-and-ipados/
- Directional only: a 14-person Braze survey (2016) attributes opt-outs to irrelevance and volume —
  https://www.braze.com/resources/articles/opt-out-of-push-notifications-why-users-do-it
- **Gaps:** no source gave a concrete "missed N times" threshold or cool-down; Todoist, TickTick, Habitica,
  Way of Life and iOS Streaks were not verified; no peer-reviewed fatigue evidence; iOS `tag` replacement
  behaviour is unconfirmed — N = 3 and one-per-run are this PRD's own decisions, not market conventions.

**Technical Context** (codebase research)
- Claim-before-send, prune `gone`, release on retryable: `src/worker/cron.ts:196-256`.
- Missed sweep runs first, its error is deferred, the miss and `missed_count` are written in one guarded batch:

- Payload builder (`title ≤ 30`, `tag`, `data.route`) and outcome classification:
  `src/shared/push-payload.ts:42-60`, `src/shared/push-outcome.ts:26-47`, `src/worker/push/send.ts:24-56`.

- Series PATCH uses a closed `EDITABLE_SERIES_FIELDS` allowlist: `src/worker/routes/series.ts:330-345`; schema

  `src/worker/db/schema.ts:87-88,180,388-419`, `src/worker/db/export-manifest.ts:46-55`.
- **Gaps:** no existing nudge, mute or "nudged at" code; the exact `src/app/` paths of the sheet's series section
